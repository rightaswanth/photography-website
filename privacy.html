1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our services. This includes:

  • Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Event Data: Event date, venue, package interest, estimated budget, and general message details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business interests, including:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide quotes for services.
  • To check and confirm our availability for your event date.
  • To process payments and fulfill our contractual obligations.
  • To send you marketing and promotional communications (if you opt-in).

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We only share your information with your consent, to comply with laws, or to fulfill business obligations. Specifically, we may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies (e.g., gallery hosting, payment processors like Stripe, client management systems) necessary to deliver our services.
  • Legal Compliance: When legally required by government authorities or law enforcement.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

We aim to protect your personal information through a system of organizational and technical security measures. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information.

5. Image Usage (Portfolio Rights)

By default, our contract includes a clause allowing us to use images and films from your event for our portfolio, website, social media, and marketing purposes. If you require strict privacy or image removal, this must be explicitly agreed upon and noted in your final contract, which may impact package pricing.
